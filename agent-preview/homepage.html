The Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games are coming

The International Olympic Committee (IOC) has elected Brisbane as the Host City of the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games. The Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games proposal involved cooperation from all levels of government and, because of that cooperation, Council and its Brisbane 2032 partners are now committed to delivering the world’s largest sporting event, which will have significant economic benefits for our residents and businesses.
